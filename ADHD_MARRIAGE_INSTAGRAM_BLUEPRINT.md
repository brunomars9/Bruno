## SECTION 2: THE REEL ANATOMY (Every Reel Follows This)

### The 60-Second How-To Reel Formula

**0:00–0:02 — THE CALLOUT HOOK**
- Avatar speaks directly to the viewer's exact pain.
- Pattern: *"If your husband [extremely specific behavior], this is for you."*
- Example: "If you've ever asked your husband the same question four times and he still missed it — save this."

**0:02–0:08 — THE PROMISE / VALIDATION**
- Name the pain by name. Tell them they're not crazy.
- Example: "You're not nagging. You're not crazy. There's actually a neurological reason this keeps happening — and a 4-word phrase that fixes it."

**0:08–0:45 — THE HOW-TO DELIVERY**
- Number it. People save numbered content.
- Use on-screen text overlays that reinforce the spoken words (kinetic captions).
- Pattern: *"Step 1... Step 2... Step 3..."* OR *"Rule 1... Rule 2... Rule 3..."*
- The avatar should pause briefly between each step (creates a "tap to rewatch" hook).

**0:45–0:55 — THE TWIST OR THE WHY**
- Add ONE deeper insight that elevates the reel beyond a tip dump.
- This is what makes it shareable. People share insight, not instructions.
- Example: "And here's the part nobody tells you — when you say it this way, you're not just communicating. You're regulating his nervous system. That's why it works."

**0:55–0:60 — THE CTA (rotates between 3 types)**

| CTA Type | Use When | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Save** | Cluster reels | "Save this for the next time it happens." |
| **DM Keyword** | Nurture reels | "Comment SCRIPTS and I'll send you 5 more like this." |
| **Follow** | Collision/Anchor reels | "I post these every Monday, Wednesday, Friday — follow so you don't lose this." |

## SECTION 3: THE PSYCHOLOGICAL DIGITAL PRODUCT FUNNEL

### Why most IG creators fail at selling digital products:
They post a reel, then say "link in bio." Conversion rate: ~0.1%.

Your avatar is a calm, trusted older-sister figure. We weaponize that the same way the Solo Aging channel does — but adapted for Instagram, where the funnel is **DM-driven**, not link-driven.

### The 3-Step DM Funnel (this is the engine):

**STEP 1 — The Trigger Reel (a Nurture reel)**
The avatar says: *"I made a free guide called The ADHD Husband Translation Guide — 12 phrases that finally got through. Comment the word GUIDE and I'll send it to you."*

**STEP 2 — The Auto-DM (use ManyChat or Instagram's native automated DMs)**
When someone comments "GUIDE," they instantly receive:

> *"Hi — sending the guide right over.*
> *Quick question before I do: what's the #1 thing about your husband's ADHD that's draining you right now? Just one word is fine — chores, communication, sex, anger, money, etc. I read every reply.*
> *— The ADHD Wife"*

Then the lead magnet PDF link arrives 30 seconds later (so they have time to reply).

**STEP 3 — The Email Sequence (after they opt in for the PDF)**
A 7-email sequence delivered over 14 days that:
- Day 1 — Validates her pain ("you're not crazy, you're exhausted")
- Day 2 — Tells the avatar's origin story (kitchen-table moment)
- Day 4 — Teaches one mini-system (a free win)
- Day 6 — Soft-launches Ebook #1 (The Survival Guide)
- Day 9 — A testimonial / case-study email
- Day 11 — A direct pitch with a 48-hour discount
- Day 14 — Last-call email + introduces Ebook #2

### The "Empathetic Interruption" inside reels (your Solo Aging trick, adapted):

For longer reels (75–90 sec) that are anchor or cluster type, at the **0:50–1:05 mark**, the avatar shifts tone slightly and delivers a 10-second soft pitch:

> *"I know this is a lot. The whole system — every script, every routine, every conversation — is in a guide I made for women in our exact situation. It's in my bio. It is not a textbook. It is what I wish I'd had five years earlier. Okay — back to it."*

**Why this works:**
- The phrase *"women in our exact situation"* — she's one of them
- *"It is not a textbook"* — removes the #1 objection
- *"What I wish I'd had five years earlier"* — sells the emotion, not the product
- *"Okay — back to it"* — proves she's not selling. She gave the info and moved on.
